# SyncGaze MCP

Connects your AI app to SyncGaze Mail, business email on your own domain. Your AI can summarise what needs a reply, find any email, draft replies, check your domain's DNS records and explain why mail is not arriving, using SyncGaze's own diagnostics and help centre.

You stay in control. The AI only drafts: every send, delete, domain or mailbox change waits for your approval on SyncGaze, and you see the exact email including every recipient. You choose which mailboxes and folders it can see, one-time codes and card numbers are masked, and you can disconnect at any time from your SyncGaze account.

The plugin adds the SyncGaze server (`https://mcp.syncgaze.in/mcp`) and a short skill that teaches the AI the safe way to use it. Sign in with your SyncGaze account when asked.

- Guide: https://mail.syncgaze.in/connect-ai/
- Privacy: https://mail.syncgaze.in/privacy/
